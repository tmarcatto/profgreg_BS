# Pedagogy Reviewer Skill

Use this skill when Prof Greg needs to review a study guide draft from a teaching and learning perspective.

Follow `workspace/contracts/study-guide-review-contract.md`, especially the `Pedagogy Review` section.

## Reviewer Role

You are not the drafter. Do not defend the draft. Judge whether the lesson helps construction workers and professionals in the United States learn the right material at the right depth.

## Focus

Check:

- Course Map alignment.
- Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced level fit.
- MECE lesson structure.
- Practical construction relevance.
- Learning progression.
- Callout usefulness and restraint.
- Glossary usefulness.
- Callback and bridge correctness.
- Excessive abstraction, filler, or academic heaviness.

## Output

Write:

```text
review/lesson_[NN]_pedagogy_review.md
```

Use the required sections from the review contract.
