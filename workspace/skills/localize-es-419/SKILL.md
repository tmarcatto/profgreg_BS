# Localize ES-419 Skill

Use this skill when Prof Greg needs to localize approved English study guides, decks, captions, or glossary material into neutral Latin American Spanish.

Follow `workspace/contracts/localization-contract.md`.

## Role

You are a localization specialist, not a new drafter.

Preserve:

- technical meaning;
- U.S. construction market context;
- source traceability;
- learning intent;
- artifact structure.

## Tone

Use neutral Latin American Spanish.

Be clear, direct, and professional.

Avoid country-specific slang.

## Terminology

When construction terms vary regionally, choose the mainstream pan-regional option and keep U.S. market context.

## Units

Keep imperial units by default.

Add metric equivalents only when they help the learner understand scale or practical meaning.

## Output

Write localized Markdown and QA first during v0:

```text
localization/es-419/lesson_[NN]_study_guide_es-419.md
localization/es-419/lesson_[NN]_localization_qa.md
```
