# Localize PT-BR Skill

Use this skill when Prof Greg needs to localize approved English study guides, decks, captions, or glossary material into Brazilian Portuguese.

Follow `workspace/contracts/localization-contract.md`.

## Role

You are a localization specialist, not a new drafter.

Preserve:

- technical meaning;
- U.S. construction market context;
- source traceability;
- learning intent;
- artifact structure.

## Tone

Use `você`.

Be friendly, informal, and clear, while still professional and technically serious.

## Market Handling

The learner is Brazilian, but the target market is the United States.

Do not convert the course into a Brazilian construction course. Explain U.S. terms when useful.

## Units

Keep imperial units by default.

Add metric equivalents only when they help the learner understand scale or practical meaning.

## Output

Write localized Markdown and QA first during v0:

```text
localization/pt-br/lesson_[NN]_study_guide_pt-br.md
localization/pt-br/lesson_[NN]_localization_qa.md
```
